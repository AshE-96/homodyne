**Description (EN), first lines:**

What happens when AI models learn from text written by other AI models? I simulated model collapse on a laptop: 1,000 generations, two toy models.
About half of new English web articles are estimated (by AI detectors) to be primarily machine-written, and the next models learn from that web.
When each generation replaced the last one's data, the rare "tails" vanished first and the distribution collapsed: 3,172 words fell to 220 by generation 100. When real data was kept and synthetic data accumulated alongside it, collapse did not happen.
Claim boundary: a toy simulation of the mechanism, not a measurement of any real model. No released model is called "collapsed" here.
Sources: Shumailov et al., Nature 2024 · Gerstgrasser et al., 2024 · Epoch AI, 2024 · Graphite, May 2026 and Oct 2025.
