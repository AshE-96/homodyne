rescored_by_us.csv — OUR arithmetic on the two JSON files above (not published by anyone):
  score(k) = accuracy - k * wrong, in points per 100 questions;
  wrong share for AA-Omniscience = hallucination_rate * (100 - accuracy) / 100 (exact, since H = i/(N-c));
  each range = the score if every rounded input is off by up to 0.5 percentage points.
  The models were not re-run: the same frozen answers are scored under k = 0, 1/3, 1, 3.
  Produced with:
    python3 artifact/penalty_threshold.py --csv artifact/derived/rescored_by_us.csv
