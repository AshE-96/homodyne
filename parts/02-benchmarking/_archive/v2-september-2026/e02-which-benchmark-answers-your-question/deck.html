Lens 2 · when the test leaks or breaks

Homodyne

The score keeps coming. The meaning doesn't.

SWE-bench Verified · coding

59.4%

of 138 audited failing tasks had flawed tests or descriptions.

  • Models reproduced the gold patch from a hint
  • Feb 2026: OpenAI stops reporting it → SWE-bench Pro

GSM1k · a fresh look-alike of GSM8K

8% max drop

for some model families on the new questions.

  • Frontier models showed little sign of overfitting
  • Contamination is a question of degree: which models, which items, how much

OpenAI, Why SWE-bench Verified no longer measures frontier coding capabilities, 23 Feb 2026 · Zhang et al., GSM1k, NeurIPS 2024, arXiv 2405.00332 v3

Lens 2 · what to do about it

Homodyne

Replace it with a better one. Not a secret one.

MMLU → MMLU-Pro

16–33% lower

accuracy with 10 options instead of 4, noisy items removed.

  • Prompt sensitivity 4–5% → 2%: harder and steadier

Public vs private test sets · 60 benchmarks

“No statistically meaningful difference.”

  • Hiding the questions did not stop saturation
  • Bigger test sets, fresh items and a written retirement rule did the work

Wang et al., MMLU-Pro, NeurIPS 2024, arXiv 2406.01574 · Akhtar et al. 2026

bluff-v1 · Stage 0

Homodyne

Bluff's chain, with the exclusions.

decision
Which model can sit behind a QA feature where a confident wrong answer costs more than “I don't know”?
costly failure
A confident fabricated answer a user acts on — worse in a language the team can't check.
evidence
Answer-or-abstain on verifiable and unanswerable questions, penalty told to the model, English and Persian.
families
calibration & abstention · factuality · multilingual & cultural · knowledge as diagnostic only
not
human preference — rewards confident style · reasoning · agents — silent on abstaining
anchor data
SimpleQA Verified — revised, but public: native Persian, unanswerable and holdout items added — E04

Claim boundary

Homodyne

What this episode does not show.

01

That the map is a taxonomy

It is a mental model. Families overlap; many benchmarks sit between two.

02

That the states are permanent

Every lifecycle state is dated: true as of 29 September 2026.

03

That saturated means useless

It still describes the past and small models. It can't choose between today's frontier systems.
