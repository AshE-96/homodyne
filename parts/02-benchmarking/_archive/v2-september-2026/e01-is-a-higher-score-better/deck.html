The mechanism

Homodyne

Under accuracy-only scoring, guessing never loses.

E[guess] = 1/365 > 0 = E[“I don't know”]

guessName a random birthdayRight once in 365 tries. Wrong costs nothing.
abstainSay “I don't know”Zero points, every time. Honesty is never rewarded.
trainOptimise for the tableA model tuned to climb it learns never to stay silent.

Kalai et al. 2025, §1 — the birthday example

The proposed fix

Homodyne

Change one number: what a wrong answer costs.

score = right − k · wrong

+1Right answerAs before.
0“I don't know”As before.
k = t / (1 − t)Wrong answerAnswer only if more than t sure. t = 0.75 → a mistake costs 3. Tell the model t.

Kalai et al. 2025, §4.2 “Explicit confidence targets”

A wrong answer costing 1/25 of a right one is enough.

At k = 1: −51 against −4. Re-scoring, not re-running — told the rule, the models might answer differently.

Definition

Homodyne

A benchmark is not a score. It is a measurement contract.

01

Decision

Which choice should this number inform?

02

Construct

The quality we want, which we can't see directly.

03

Items

Which questions, from where, in which language.

04

Protocol

Prompt, settings, repeats, who grades.

05

Scoring

What earns a point. What costs one.

06

Boundary

What the result may not be used to say.

The number in the table is the output of all six. Miss one, and you don't know what it says.

What this episode does not show.

Which model is better today

That one rule is correct

The answer

Homodyne

A higher score means a better model. Only sometimes

A higher score is a better answer to the question the scoring rule asks.

If that is not your question, it is not your score.
