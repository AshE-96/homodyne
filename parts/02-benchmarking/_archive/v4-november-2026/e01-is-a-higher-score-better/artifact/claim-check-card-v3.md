# Six questions before using a benchmark score

1. **Decision:** Which actual choice will this evidence inform?
2. **Task:** What observable work does the benchmark ask the model to do?
3. **Data:** Which items, sources, languages and base-question clusters are represented?
4. **Run:** Which versions, prompts, tools, budgets, failures and repeats produced the outputs?
5. **Score:** What are the numerator, denominator, error cost and missing-data rule?
6. **Boundary:** What population and use does the claim cover, with what uncertainty?

Separate marginal CI overlap is not a decision rule for paired differences.
Rescoring fixed responses is not evidence of changed model behaviour.
