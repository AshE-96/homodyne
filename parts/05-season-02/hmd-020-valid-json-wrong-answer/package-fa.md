The AI's form is perfectly tidy. So why is the answer wrong? Valid JSON means the record parses and fits the schema, not that the amount or the date is right.

We built an invented grocery ("Sabad-e Mahalleh", everything synthetic) and 100 Persian chat orders full of familiar traps: toman vs rial (a factor of 10), "450 toman" meaning 450 thousand, amounts in words, Arabic-Indic digits, "the day after tomorrow", Jalali dates, "a 3-to-5-kilo watermelon", and values the customer never gave. We compare three ways of asking: free JSON, the schema in the prompt, and the provider's native structured output; then decide, as a product manager, which orders are recorded automatically, which get one repair attempt, and which go to a person.

Local run (no model, synthetic data): a date shifted by one day passed both the schema and the rules, 87 of 87. Ten-fold amounts and Jalali dates in the Gregorian slot were caught by the rules.
