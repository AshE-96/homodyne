### English (dub / site)

```
What happens when a hundred people ask the AI at once? A demo is fast for one person because nobody is in the queue. We load an AI service from one user to a hundred and watch the queue, the wait and the errors live.

First we write the promise in one sentence (the SLO): 95% of requests see the first word within 3 s, and fewer than 1% fail. Then we build a simulated service with four "tellers" and a queue, and load it with 1, 10, 50 and 100 virtual users and with fixed request rates. Then the same test on a small real model (qwen2.5:1.5b in Ollama) on one laptop. Finally, as a product manager, we decide when to bound the queue, when to shorten answers, and when to buy capacity.

Simulated service (illustrative numbers): from 10 to 50 users, the 95th-percentile time to first word went from 0.43 s to about 16 s (59 and 224 requests).

The laptop numbers belong to one laptop, one model, one Ollama setting and one load pattern, and are not comparable with any other server. Answer quality was not measured. We only loaded services running on our own computer; do not load-test anyone else's service without permission.

### Short 2 · «صد کاربر یعنی صد درخواست در ثانیه؟» (حدود ۴۵ ثانیه)
