## ۴. English description (copy-ready)

```
Do we need the most expensive AI for every question? "Convert toman to rial" and "a child's dose from a leaflet" are not the same kind of request.
We put a small, cheap model and a large, expensive one side by side, with several routers between them, on 200 requests with known answers.

Like a clinic: the GP first, and the specialist only when needed. 200 Persian and English requests in five kinds (simple facts, unit conversions, extracting a field from a short text, multi-step word problems, and a high-risk kind: dose questions answered from a fictional leaflet). Each router's threshold is chosen on 64 non-medication requests (of 80) and scored once on 96 frozen ones (of 120). Answers are graded against the truth, not by an AI judge. The decision uses cost per successful task, the slowest answer in every twenty (p95), the share escalated, and two kinds of routing error. The high-risk kind is outside the curve by design: always the big model plus a human check.

Claim boundary: the requests and texts are invented (ILLUSTRATIVE); the medicines do not exist and nothing here is medical advice. Any result holds only for these 200 requests, these two models, this date and these prices; prices change. RouteLLM's and FrugalGPT's numbers are their results on their own benchmarks and models, not a prediction of ours. ### Short 1 · «سختی سؤال توی صورت‌حساب نیست» (حدود ۵۰ ثانیه)
```

- **هوک ۲ ثانیه‌ای:** «تومن به ریال، به قیمت متخصص؟» · متن روی صفحه: `Simple question, specialist price?`
- **زمان در ویدیوی اصلی:** 00:50–01:45 (درمانگاه و «تا حدود صد برابر») و 07:05–07:51 (هزینهٔ هر کار موفق).
- **متن:**
  تومن به ریال، به قیمت متخصص؟
  مدل‌های هوش مصنوعی به ازای تکه‌های متن پول می‌گیرن، که بهش می‌گن توکن؛ نه به ازای سختی سؤال.
  هرچند مدلی که قبل از جواب فکر می‌کنه، برای سؤال سخت متن بیشتری می‌نویسه.
  یه مقاله به اسم FrugalGPT دیده بود قیمت این سرویس‌ها می‌تونه تا حدود صد برابر با هم فرق کنه.
  مثل درمانگاه: برای سرماخوردگی اول پزشک عمومی، و فقط اگه لازم بود، متخصص.
