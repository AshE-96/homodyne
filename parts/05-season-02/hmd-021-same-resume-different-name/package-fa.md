# HMD-021 · بستهٔ انتشار

**کلیدواژهٔ اصلی:** AI و رزومه / تبعیض در استخدام · **کلیدواژه‌های فرعی:** غربالگری رزومه با هوش مصنوعی، ChatGPT برای استخدام، سوگیری جنسیتی AI، آزمون جفتی، ممیزی سوگیری

> **قاعدهٔ این بسته:** تا اجرای واقعی (`run` + `report`)، هیچ عنوان، تامبنیل، توضیح، Short یا پستی نمی‌گوید مدل «تبعیض قائل شد» یا «نشد». همه‌چیز باید با هر سه نتیجه درست بماند: فرق ثابت شد، فرقی ثابت نشد، بی‌فرقی (داخل مرز) ثابت شد. جاهایی که پس از اجرا عوض می‌شوند [RUN] دارند.

```
Does AI treat two identical résumés differently? When an AI does the first pass on job applications, a single résumé can never tell you whether the name changed the outcome.
In this episode we build a paired audit: everything fixed, only the name changes.

50 pairs of synthetic Persian résumés for three Tehran jobs (product analyst, pharmacy assistant, accountant). Within a pair the skills are identical and only the first name differs (common male and female names from Iran's civil-registration statistics), plus a name-blind version. Military-service status, age, graduation year, photo and marital status are removed; because a missing military-service line is itself a cue on a man's résumé, a version stating completed service is measured separately. The model does two tasks: "which of these two should we interview?" and "score this résumé from 1 to 10". Every pair is shown in both orders so that a preference for the first résumé (position bias) is measured separately. Every question is asked three times; the unit of analysis is the résumé pair. The decision rule was written and registered before any run.
The final decision: may this system do first-pass screening, or only assist a human reviewer?

Claim boundary: a limited audit; 50 synthetic résumé pairs, one model, one prompt, one date, and only the gender signal of a first name. It does not show the model's intent and is not a verdict on real hiring. "No difference found" does not mean "fair". The outside studies mentioned used other models or human employers, in other countries and years, in English. The New York City law is mentioned for comparison only, not as legal advice.
```
