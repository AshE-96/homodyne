Derived files for HMD-009 (Midtown Manhattan -> JFK Airport, yellow taxi, March-April 2026).

midtown_jfk_trips.csv  one row per kept trip: pickup time, trip minutes, and two live "neighbour" signals
                       measured at that pickup time (median mph and count of other taxi trips that ENDED in the
                       15 minutes before it: started in Midtown going elsewhere / ended at JFK coming from elsewhere).
hourly_profile.csv     weekday departure hour -> trips, 10th percentile, median, 90th percentile of trip minutes.
results.json           output of `python3 nyc_eta.py race --json` (default split 2026-04-13).

Source: NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (TLC), TLC Trip Record Data, Yellow Taxi Trip Records (PARQUET).

Field meanings (TLC data dictionary, 18 Mar 2025): tpep_pickup/dropoff_datetime = "when the meter was
engaged/disengaged"; PULocationID/DOLocationID = TLC Taxi Zone where the meter was engaged/disengaged;
trip_distance = miles reported by the taximeter.
Licence / terms: public data published by the City of New York (NYC Open Data Law: public data sets are made
available without restrictions on use; the City may ask users to identify the source). TLC: "makes no
representations as to the accuracy of these data". Credit line: "Data: NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (TLC)".

Zones: Midtown = TLC zones 161 Midtown Center, 162 Midtown East, 163 Midtown North, 164 Midtown South,
230 Times Sq/Theatre District. JFK = zone 132 JFK Airport.
Cleaning (ours): pickups 2026-03-01 .. 2026-04-30; route trips kept if 12-25 miles and 10-180 minutes
(11,809 of 12,159 Midtown -> JFK records). Neighbour trips: 0.5-30 mi / 2-120 min (from Midtown) and
0.5-40 mi / 2-150 min (to JFK).

Exact processing (run from the repo root):
  python3 -I parts/03-standalone/hmd-009-maps-eta/artifact/nyc_eta.py build \
      --raw parts/03-standalone/hmd-009-maps-eta/artifact/cache/tlc --out parts/03-standalone/hmd-009-maps-eta/artifact/derived
  python3 -I parts/03-standalone/hmd-009-maps-eta/artifact/nyc_eta.py race --json
(build needs the installed duckdb Python module, used here at 1.5.5; race is standard library only.)
Every predictor, error and percentile in these files is Homodyne's own computation on the TLC records.
