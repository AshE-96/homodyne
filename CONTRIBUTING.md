# Contributing

Homodyne is made by one narrator, but corrections and ideas are welcome.

## Corrections

The channel's promise is that every number has a source. If one does not, or the source says something else:

1. Open a **Correction** issue with the episode (HMD id) or post URL, the line or timestamp, and a link to the primary source.
2. Confirmed corrections are fixed in the files here, noted with a date in the episode's `sources.md`, and, if the video is already published, added as a pinned comment and an on-screen card in the description.

## Ideas

Open an **Episode idea** issue: a familiar question, and how it could be measured (dataset, demo, or primary source).

## Changing episode files

- Follow [`channel/season-01/episode-bible-fa.md`](channel/season-01/episode-bible-fa.md).
- `make episodes` must pass (beats, word counts, decks, links).
- Never state a number that has not been sourced or run; tag it `[VERIFY]` or `[RUN]` until it is.
- Blog posts follow [`site/BLOG-GUIDE.md`](site/BLOG-GUIDE.md).
